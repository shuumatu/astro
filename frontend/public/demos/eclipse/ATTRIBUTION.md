# Eclipse observer assets

| File | Source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `moon-nearside.webp` | NASA Scientific Visualization Studio [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), LROC WAC colour mosaic already held in `../moon/moon-color-4k.webp` | Orthographic near-side surface; shadow and colour are applied by the live illustration. |
| `corona-2024.webp` | [NASA photograph](https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/2024-total-solar-eclipse-4/) by Keegan Barber, 8 April 2024 | Transparent corona appearance only. The photographed Moon is removed; the selected event controls both discs and their overlap. |

NASA imagery is used for educational illustration, with attribution and without implying NASA endorsement. The photographic corona is not a recording or prediction of the selected future eclipse. Rebuild with `tools/eclipse-images/build-observer-assets.py` (Pillow).
